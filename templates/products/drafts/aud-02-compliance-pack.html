What it does

Compliance as a standing practice.

Mapping controls to evidence — and re-assembling the evidence every month — is specialist work that rots when it's nobody's job. The Compliance Pack makes it somebody's job: a scoping workshop maps your controls to platform records, evidence templates show exactly what each month's proof looks like, and a named reviewer assembles and walks the pack with you monthly.

Controls with no platform evidence are named as gaps with a manual-collection procedure — never hidden. Platform changes that affect your mapped controls are flagged before they take effect.

How it works

1

Map

The setup workshop maps your controls to the platform records that satisfy them — audit exports, KYA records, dispute rulings, stake-ledger entries. Gaps are named explicitly.

2

Template

Each control gets an evidence template: what the monthly proof looks like and where it comes from, so the pack is reproducible.

3

Review

Every month your reviewer assembles the cumulative evidence pack, walks it with you, and logs each control as satisfied, gap-open, or gap-closed.

What if a control has no platform evidence?
It is named as a gap in your control map, with a manual-collection procedure defined at setup. Gaps are tracked month to month until closed.
