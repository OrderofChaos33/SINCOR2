What it does

Operations with a name on it.

Running many deployments turns operations into tribal knowledge and missed alerts. Enterprise Mesh replaces that with a service: every deployment onboarded the same way through a runbook — KYA confirmed, stake posture reviewed, audit trail on, drift watch configured, alert routing proven with a fired-and-received test before sign-off.

Your named SINCOR operator keeps one operating picture across your deployments, reviews it with you weekly, and responds to incidents on defined times: one hour for critical, one business day for standard.

How it works

1

Incidents get acknowledged on SLA, triaged with the audit trail at hand, and driven through resolution with a written monthly operations summary.
