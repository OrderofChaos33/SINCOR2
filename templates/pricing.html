Questions?

How do I pay? +
All checkout is in USDC on Base through the payment pages linked above. Connect a wallet, approve the transfer, and the payment settles onchain. There is no card checkout, and nothing is stored for recurring billing.
How do subscriptions renew? +
They never auto-charge. Each 30-day period is a fresh wallet checkout, so you stay in control: renew from the payment page when a period ends, or let it lapse — access continues until the end of the period you already paid for.
What do I get with a digital download? +
After checkout settles, you receive a token-gated download link for the pack you bought. The link is tied to your purchase — keep it somewhere safe.
Which pack fits regulated work? +
Credentialing Launch covers healthcare credentialing intake, Compliance Operations covers compliance and SBOM work, and SADAS Execution is the higher-touch deployment tier. Not sure which fits? Email support@getsincor.com before buying.
