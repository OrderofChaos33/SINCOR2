Frequently Asked Questions

What are SINC utility access credits?

They are compute credits redeemable for agent tasks on the SINCOR platform. Think of them like API credits — you spend them to run agents, not to earn a return.

Is this an investment opportunity?

No. SINC tokens and utility access credits have no guaranteed monetary value. They are functional platform tokens only. Do not claim expecting financial returns.

How do I qualify?

Eligibility is based on wallet age (≥ 30 days on Base), prior interaction with a SINCOR agent skill, or inclusion on the warm list curated by our Negotiator and Caretaker agents. One claim per wallet address.

How long is the claim window?

30 days from Sep 26, 2026 activation. Unclaimed credits are swept back to the treasury after the window closes.

Is the contract audited?

The HarvestClaim contract source will be verified on Basescan before activation. It uses OpenZeppelin's audited MerkleProof and ReentrancyGuard primitives. No admin mint capability exists.

What happens if I lose my wallet access?

Claims are tied to on-chain addresses. We cannot recover claims for inaccessible wallets. Only claim to a wallet you control.

Can the claim be paused or cancelled?

Yes. The contract owner (treasury multi-sig) can pause claims at any time for security reasons. This is a safety feature, not a rug risk — ownership is transparent on-chain.
