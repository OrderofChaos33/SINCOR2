How recovery works

Three steps, no key export.

Step 1

Open the right wallet

Unlock MetaMask and switch to Account 6 — the wallet that placed the hook floor. Only a signature from that wallet can authorise its cancellation.

Step 2

Verify the hook above

Match the live hook address and chain in the status panel against the position you intend to cancel. If the panel cannot load, stop and retry later — do not sign blind.

Step 3

Sign the cancellation

Sign the floor-cancel message in MetaMask. Signing is not a transaction: it moves no funds and costs no gas, and the signature is produced inside the wallet.

Safety: SINCOR will never ask for your private key or seed phrase — not on this page, not by email, not anywhere. Anyone who does is not SINCOR. Recovery works by MetaMask signature only, precisely so no private key export is ever required.
