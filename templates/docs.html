Operations console

After a paid plan (or an operator session) the console lives at /dashboard.

  • Overview — live KPIs stay blank until the swarm bus reports real numbers.
  • Agents — roster sized to the plan: Starter 10, Professional 25, Enterprise / Operator 42.
  • Activity — recorded agent actions. Empty until tasks actually run.
  • Training Vault — guides for the active subscription. Operators open it without a customer order.
Staff sign in at /login, then use /admin and /command-center for the swarm feed. Deploy Agent on an operator session opens the command center, not a product SKU.
