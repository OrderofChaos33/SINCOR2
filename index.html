MoebiusX — owner action 2: send & swap (REAL FUNDS) — phone version

Open this page in your normal phone browser. Tap Connect and choose Rainbow in the WalletConnect popup — Rainbow opens so you can approve the connection, the Step-1 transaction, and the Step-2 signature. This page never asks for the typed-data signing route and never asks for any private key.

What this is: yesterday the agent wallet's spare ETH was sent out to your own wallet (Option B). The goal was the other outcome — that ETH as USDC inside the agent wallet, where the trader can use it. This page finishes that conversion in two taps inside your wallet app, nothing else:

  • Step 1 moves from your wallet into the agent wallet — a plain transaction this page asks your wallet to send. You approve it in Rainbow like any other send.
  • Step 2 signs the swap of to USDC inside the agent wallet — one personal_sign over a prepared operation. The USDC it buys — never less than (build-time spot − 1%; the operation reverts instead of filling below that) — lands in the agent wallet itself.
  • The agent wallet keeps a 0.0007 ETH gas reserve — that part of the balance is never swapped.

Nothing moves when you sign in Step 2 — the signature is off-chain; the operator submits the operation afterwards, and only after re-checking on-chain that the Step-1 transfer landed exactly and the operation number is unchanged. If anything has drifted, the operator's relay refuses and submits nothing.

Base Mainnet only (chain 8453). This page works with real ETH on Base. The only external code this page loads is the WalletConnect connection library and the ethers signature-check library (both from the unpkg CDN) — used solely to reach your wallet and to check your signature against your wallet address on this page as a first look only. Balance checks read the Base public RPC named in the packet. Neither can change the figures below. Whatever Rainbow returns is kept — this page never deletes a signature; the copy box unlocks the moment a signature comes back, and the operator re-checks everything server-side before anything is submitted.
