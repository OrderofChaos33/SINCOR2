## Legal doctrine (non-negotiable)

Everything in this playbook must be legal and compliant. Hard bans:

- No unauthorized access (hacking, credential stuffing, scraping behind auth).
- No sabotage, no disinformation, no defamation, no fake reviews.
- No market manipulation, no wash trading, no spoofing.
- No antitrust violations (no price-fixing, no market allocation with competitors).
- No misappropriation of trade secrets, no inducing breach of contract.
- No impersonation of competitors, regulators, or customers.

What "offensive" means here: **speed, excellence, and strategic positioning
so aggressive it functions as offense** — out-execute, out-price, out-hire,
out-partner, out-ship. The most devastating legal weapon is a better machine
running faster than anyone can follow.
